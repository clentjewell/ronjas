Our FAQ

Fees & policies

Founded on principles of holistic well-being, we strive to support mind, body, and spirit through a personalised approach to healing. Below are answers to common questions about our services.

What does a session cost?
Studio sessions in Banora Point are $200, and home visits within 20 km are $220. Introductory offers are listed on the events page.
How long is a session?
Allow around 90 minutes. Sessions are unhurried and are never cut short.
Where do sessions take place?
At the Banora Point studio, or in your own home if you are within roughly 20 km.
How do I book or cancel?
Call 0403 835 018 or use the enquiry form. Please give as much notice as you can if you need to change a booking.
Is this a substitute for medical care?
No. This work sits alongside medical and psychological care, and does not replace it.
