Our Approach

Emotional healing is a journey, not a destination. Our mission is to support you in processing and releasing emotional burdens, allowing for a deeper sense of peace and alignment.

Rooted in ancient Egyptian practices, this technique harnesses the power of essential oils and intentional touch to awaken your innate healing abilities. Whether you seek emotional release, clarity, or a deeper spiritual connection, each session is intuitively tailored to your needs.

Egyptian Emotional Clearing Technique

This sacred practice blends therapeutic-grade essential oils with intuitive bodywork to help surface and release deeply held emotions. Rooted in ancient Egyptian traditions, this technique creates a deeply meditative and nurturing experience that allows you to let go of past emotional burdens. Through the power of scent and gentle touch, it promotes emotional balance, relaxation, and a greater connection to your inner self.

Deep Emotional Healing

Unresolved emotions can manifest as stress, anxiety, or a sense of being stuck. This technique supports the gentle release of emotional weight stored in the body, allowing for profound inner healing. By accessing the limbic system—the part of the brain that processes emotions—this practice helps you gain clarity, process past experiences, and move forward with a renewed sense of peace and purpose.

Essential Oil Education

Essential oils have been used for centuries to support emotional and spiritual well-being. In this practice, we use Young Living therapeutic-grade essential oils, selected for their ability to gently release emotional blockages and promote relaxation. Each oil blend is chosen intuitively, supporting your unique emotional state and helping you achieve greater harmony, calm, and mental clarity.

Inner Alignment & Balance

When emotions become imbalanced, they can disrupt mental clarity, decision-making, and overall well-being. This session is designed to realign your energy and bring you back into a state of balance. Through deep relaxation and mindful application of essential oils, you will experience a heightened state of awareness, helping you reconnect with your intuition and inner wisdom.

Sacred Rituals for Transformation

Inspired by ancient Egyptian healing rituals, this practice is designed to support spiritual growth and transformation. Historically performed on priests, priestesses, and initiates before major transitions, this technique helps release emotional patterns that no longer serve you. By clearing away stagnant energy and emotional blocks, you create space for new possibilities, empowerment, and deeper spiritual connection.

This session provides a sanctuary where you can completely unwind, allowing stored tension and anxiety to dissolve. Fees & Policies

Founded on principles of holistic well-being, we strive to support mind, body, and spirit through a personalized approach to healing. Below are answers to common questions about our services.

What is the Egyptian Emotional Clearing Technique (EECT)?

EECT is a sacred healing practice that combines therapeutic-grade essential oils with intuitive bodywork. It helps release stored emotions, reduce stress, and restore emotional and spiritual balance.

What should I expect during a session?

Each session is a deeply personal experience. You’ll be anointed with essential oils and guided into a meditative state, allowing emotions to surface and release naturally. The session is silent, creating a safe and nurturing space for transformation.

How long does a session last?

A typical EECT session lasts around 60 to 90 minutes.

What are the benefits of this technique?

This technique can help with emotional release, stress reduction, trauma healing, mental clarity, and deep relaxation. Many clients experience a renewed sense of peace and connection to their inner selves.
